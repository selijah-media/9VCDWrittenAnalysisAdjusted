Packaging Analysis Rubric

CriteriaSatisfactoryNot Satisfactory
1. Introduction and DescriptionIntroduction and description cover the main features of the packaging.Introduction and description are vague, unclear or missing; key features are not identified.
2. Understanding of Context and Purpose/sShows an understanding of the packaging’s purpose and context.Limited understanding of purpose and context; lacks clear connections.
3. Understanding of the Target AudienceTarget audience is acknowledged with some detail and understanding.Limited recognition of the target audience; lacks detail.
4. Evaluation of Design ElementsDesign elements are evaluated with some relevance to audience attraction.Limited evaluation of design elements; lacks connection to audience engagement.
5. Evaluation of Design PrinciplesDesign principles are understood, with some connection to the audience.Limited understanding of design principles; weak or unclear connections.
6. Understanding of Methods, Media, and MaterialsMethods, media and materials are understood and the analysis is basic.Limited understanding of methods, media and materials; analysis is minimal.
7. Grammar, Sentence Structure, and Design LanguageGrammar and structure are generally clear, with minor errors; design language is basic.Frequent grammar errors and weak structure; limited design language used.
