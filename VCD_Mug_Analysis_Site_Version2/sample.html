Sample response: Natural Wonders of the Sea – T2

Paragraph 1 – Introduction to the Visual Communication Design

The ‘Natural Wonders of the Sea’ mug by T2 is a beautifully designed visual communication piece. T2, the client, required a packaging design that would protect the mug and promote their new ocean-inspired collection. The packaging is captivating and well-structured: vibrant illustrations of sea creatures and coral wrap around the box, the brand name ‘T2’ is placed at the top, and the product name is positioned near the centre.

Paragraph 2 – Context & Main Purpose

The context is retail, with the packaging displayed in T2 stores and online. The carrier is a sturdy cardboard packaging net. The main purposes are to promote the T2 brand and its new collection, to identify the product as part of the ‘Natural Wonders of the Sea’ series, and to advertise the intricate, ocean-themed design that appeals to nature lovers.

Paragraph 3 – Characteristics of the Target Audience

The target audience is diverse. The age range spans from young adults (18+) to mature tea enthusiasts, and the gender is not specific. People with an interest in sustainable living, oceanic themes and artistic homeware would be drawn to the design. The socio-economic status is generally middle to upper class because T2 products are premium. The location is mostly urban and suburban areas near T2 stores, and the cultural background is broad, appealing to those who appreciate global art influences and marine conservation themes.

Paragraph 4 – Dominant Design Elements

Design Element 1: Colour: Vibrant blues, turquoise and hints of coral orange mirror the natural hues of the ocean, evoking calmness and a connection to nature.

Design Element 2: Line: Delicate linework illustrates intricate coral designs and ocean waves, creating a sense of movement and reinforcing the oceanic theme.

Paragraph 5 – Dominant Design Principles

Design Principle 1: Balance (Symmetrical): Illustrations are evenly distributed around the packaging, creating visual stability and harmony.

Design Principle 2: Pattern (Repetition): Repeated coral patterns and marine life illustrations create visual consistency and rhythm, reinforcing the underwater theme.

Paragraph 6 – Methods, Media and Materials

The design uses the method of digital image manipulation and digital design page layout. The media used was vector-based programs (e.g. Adobe Illustrator – lines and shapes), used for the sea-creature and coral shapes. As digital media was used, the material was the screen.
